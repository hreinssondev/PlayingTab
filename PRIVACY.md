# PlayingTab Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 12, 2026_

PlayingTab does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

To find the tab that is playing audio or video, the extension checks whether media is playing on the web pages you open. Only a yes/no "playing" flag and a timestamp are kept, in Safari's local extension storage on your Mac, and they are cleared when Safari restarts. Page content, URLs, and browsing history are never read, recorded, or sent anywhere.

PlayingTab makes no network requests and contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
