# PlayingTab Privacy Policy

_Effective October 12, 2026_

**PlayingTab does not collect, store, or share any personal information.**

## How it works

To find the tab that is playing audio or video, PlayingTab notes whether media is playing on the pages you have open. This status stays on your Mac, in Safari’s extension storage, and is cleared when Safari quits. PlayingTab never reads page content, addresses, or browsing history.

## No data leaves your device

PlayingTab makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking.
